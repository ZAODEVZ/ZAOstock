Olive-400 on paper3.1:1 · large text and UI only
Red-300 on ink5.5:1 · AA · red on the inverse card

Ratios computed from the hex values with the WCAG 2 relative-luminance formula on 2026-08-27, rounded to one decimal. Two of Candy's defaults were raised to pass: the button ground (red-500 to red-600) and the secondary and muted ink mixes (62% and 46% to 70% and 66%).
