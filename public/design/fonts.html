Why three families

The day

One venue at a time. Outdoors, then in.

The parklet runs from noon until six. Then the whole street walks next door.

Forty-five minute sets with a DJ covering every changeover, so the music never stops for long. At four the stage resets for WaveWarZ: two artists go head to head and the audience decides, in the street and online.
