# Seconds map - who covers a lead while the lead is on stage

> **Zaal, 31 August 2026:** "all of the official leads when they are performing
> can be given to the seconds."

Every lead on this day also performs. That is not a staffing gap, it is a
scheduling conflict - the same person cannot run the desk and be on the stage in
front of it. One rule solves it: **every lead has a named second, and the second
holds the role for exactly the window the lead is performing.**

This replaces hiring for the two roles the people map lists as open
(sound during WaveWarZ, AV during Dcoop's set). It does not replace the roles
that are genuinely unfilled - stage manager, First Aid contact, street pointer.

## What this means in practice

**Dcoop needs one second, not two.** The same person covers his 13:15-13:45 set
and his fire slot. Both are short and both are on the outdoor stage, so one name
covers roughly 45 minutes of the day.

**Stilo needs a second for four straight hours**, 16:00-20:00 - through WaveWarZ
and his own DJ set. This is the largest single gap on the day and the one worth
solving first. Note the 18:00 move happens inside that window, so the second also
carries the handover from outdoor to indoor.

**Katina already covers Steve twice** and this should be said out loud rather than
assumed. Q22 in the run of show asks who is on Black Moon's door at 18:00 - if the
answer is Steve, it conflicts with his own 20:00 set two hours later.

**Aziz and Ohnahji do not perform**, so the livestream split needs no seconds.
Aziz holds the rig, encoder, bitrate and destinations; Ohnahji holds scheduling,
guests and virtual segments.
