# ZAOstock run of show, 3 October 2026 - the whole day in 5-minute rows

Built 27 August 2026 for the grill. Every row is five minutes, 06:00 to close.
A dot means the row above continues. Every unknown is a numbered question
in the list first, then attached to the row where it bites, so the day can be
walked slot by slot and each question answered by typing a number, a name, a
time or yes/no. Nothing here is a figure Zaal has not typed: the grid is
Steve's 45/15 cadence from noon (Zaal, 27 Aug), WaveWarZ is locked 16:00-18:00,
the move is 18:00, the evening is in booking. The WaveWarZ bracket shape is
the working doc's proposal, not agreed. Sunset 18:12 is my arithmetic.

Companion: `docs/plans/production-plan-2026-10-03.md` (the why) and
`docs/plans/zaal-only-PRODUCTION.md` (the twelve Zaal answers first).

## Arithmetic that does not move

- 12:00-16:00 = 240 min = 4 x 45 + 4 x 15. Zero slack. Every minute of intro,
  mic break or story segment (Q13, Q18, Q21) comes out of a set.
- Five confirmed acts, four slots. Q15 is the decision; Q19 and Q20 decide
  whether option 2 (three at 30) is even available.
- 16:00-18:00 is locked. 18:00 is locked. Nothing after 16:00 moves with the daytime.
- Six things have no slot anywhere: the fifth act (Q15), the intro (Q13), the
  sponsor break (Q18), the WaveWarZ story (Q21), the fire spinning (Q27), and
  Fellenz's indoor 30 (Q35).
