# ZAOstock run of show, 3 October 2026 - the whole day in 5-minute rows (v4)

Built 27 August 2026 for the grill. v4 is rebuilt from the confirmed record in
`~/zao-vault/daily/2026-08-27.md` (grill rounds 1-3 and the 20:4x running order,
confirmed by the orchestrator's 20:46 RETRO-LOG): a 5-minute intro at noon; the
order Crown Vics / Sen / Dcoop / Fellenz at about 30 minutes each, Fellenz ending
before WaveWarZ; no DJ - every gap is the MC plus sponsor spots; Zaal assumes
5-minute changeovers where the plan's rule is 15 (Q1, the first question); the
evening is Stilo's DJ set 18:00-19:30 and then one of Steve's acts to close.
Werb is not fully confirmed and wanted for WaveWarZ; Lyons Den is confirmed and
not in the order. On 30-minute sets the afternoon has an OPEN STRETCH of about
70 minutes before 16:00 - Zaal's dream slots (JANGO, Jadyn, Hurricane) if time.
Every row is five minutes, 08:00 to close, with Friday night as the first line.
A dot means the row above continues. Sunset 18:12 is my arithmetic.

Companion: `docs/plans/production-plan-2026-10-03.md` (the why) and
`docs/plans/zaal-only-PRODUCTION.md` (the twelve Zaal answers first).

## Arithmetic that does not move

- 12:00-16:00 = 240 min. Intro 5 + four sets at ~30 = 125. With 15-minute
  changeovers (three) the four acts end at 14:50 and 70 minutes are open; with
  5-minute changeovers they end at 14:20 and 100 are open. Q1 decides which.
- The open stretch is not a problem, it is the dream: JANGO, Jadyn, Hurricane if
  time, and a home for Lyons Den (confirmed) and maybe Werb. Q3, Q4, Q5.
- 16:00-18:00 is locked and opens with the story. 18:00 is locked. Stilo
  18:00-19:30 is decided. From 19:30 the night belongs to one of Steve's acts,
  all still PROPOSED - Q6 is the evening's one question.
- Three things have no slot anywhere: the fire spinning (Q21), Fellenz's indoor
  30 (Q26), and a separate sponsor break (moot if every gap carries spots).
