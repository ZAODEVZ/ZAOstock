# ZAOstock production plan - Saturday 3 October 2026

Ellsworth, Maine. Written 26 August 2026, 38 days out.

Steve, this is the whole day on paper, built on the cadence you proposed:
45-minute sets, 15-minute changeovers, a DJ covering the gaps. Everything you
offered is in here with a name against it. The four things I need back from you
are at the bottom.

One correction up front, because your list assumes something we changed.

---

## 1. One stage at a time, not two

**The day runs outdoors on the Franklin Street Parklet until 6pm, then the whole
thing walks next door into Black Moon and stays there.** Not two live stages
running at once.

This changed on 23 August and the public schedule at zaostock.com/program was
rebuilt for it. Your supply list reads as though both stages are live all day,
which is why some of the numbers below differ from what you were picturing.

Why we made the call:

- **One crowd.** The parklet is a street. If Black Moon is also live at 2pm,
  people have to choose, and 200 people split across two rooms feels like 100 in
  each. Kept together, the street looks full, which is the whole point of doing
  this outdoors on Franklin Street.
- **One PA crew at a time.** We do not have two sound crews. We barely have one
  that is fully spoken for. Running two live stages means two operators, two
  setups and two strikes, on a day that already starts at 6am.
- **The move is the moment.** At six the music stops outside and everyone walks
  into your bar together. That is a better handoff than a slow leak of people
  who were already indoors.

What it costs us, and I want to be straight about both:

- **Changeovers are now audible.** Your alternating design existed so the music
  never stopped. With one stage there is a real 15-minute gap between every act.
  Your DJ is the fix, and that is the single most useful thing on your list.
- **Black Moon takes no daytime footfall by default,** because everyone is
  outside from noon to six. That hits your bar percentage and it hits the
  local-lift number we said we would measure. The idea from 15 August of putting
  the stream up on screens inside is still the right fix and it is on us to
  build it.

## 5. Open items for you

Four things, in the order they block us.

**1. The PA handoff at 6pm.**
This is the biggest unanswered question on the whole day. When you say sound and
PA on each stage, we need to know whether that is one system that moves or two
systems that exist independently, who strikes the outdoor rig, who runs the
indoor one, and whether it is the same operator doing both. On a day that runs one
stage at a time, a single system that moves is cheaper and simpler, but only if
somebody is being paid to move it while the crowd walks. If it is two systems,
that is fine too, we just need to know now.

Alongside that, the scope: gear list, channel count, who sets up and who
strikes, who operates, what power it needs, price, and what happens if it falls
through. We also need a date by which it is confirmed, so that if it is not, we
start a backup that day rather than a week later. We are not asking you to
guarantee anything, we are asking for the date.

**2. Porta potties, count and a handicap unit.**
You are covering one at the city parking lot and suggested one or two more,
maybe an accessible unit. Our read is four total: your one, two more standard,
and one ADA accessible. For a free public street event that is the number that
does not embarrass us. What we need from you is who books it, since you know the
local vendor, and whether Black Moon is covering any beyond the first. We are
ready to pay for the rest.

**3. Wristbands or lanyards for basement access.**
Agreed, and your rule is right, the basement is a dressing room and not a
hangout. Proposal: we buy them and bring them, you tell us who gets one. Our
count is the performer headcount from item 3 above plus about six crew. Confirm
that works and whether you want your own staff carrying one too.

**4. North Creek at 6-9 versus our 6-8 block.**
Thank you for underwriting this. It does change one thing. Our run of show had a
DJ set from 6 to 8 and local acts after. North Creek at 6 to 9 is better, so
here is what we propose: the room fills from 6 to 6:30, North Creek runs 6:30 to
9, and our DJ closes the night from 9. Say the word if North Creek needs a
straight 6 o'clock start and we will move the fill window.

## 7. The one measurement that cannot be taken afterwards

The stated point of this day is showing what an event like this does for local
businesses. That means an average Saturday compared against 3 October, and after
3 October nobody can go back and find out what a normal Saturday looked like.

Steve, the ask we talked about: roughly what does a normal Saturday take at
Black Moon, and what did 3 October take. Ranges are completely fine, and we
publish it combined across businesses rather than per business unless you want
the credit. Two other downtown businesses will get the same question. This is
the only thing on the whole board whose window shuts permanently on 3 October.
