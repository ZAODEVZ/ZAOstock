## Review of the run of show, Aug 24

*Read the whole day end to end and looked for what breaks. In rough order of how badly it bites.*

### 1. Five-minute changeovers are not real

**The grid gives 5 minutes between acts.** *That is drum-kit-and-amps time, not band-swap time. A full changeover is usually 10 to 20.*

**Why it matters more than it looks:** the outdoor block totals **exactly 240 minutes with zero slack**, and WaveWarZ starts at 4 regardless. *One act running eight minutes over does not cost eight minutes, it eats the next changeover and cascades.*

**Two ways out.** *Shared backline - one kit and amps stay up all day, acts swap only guitars and pedals, which genuinely makes 5 minutes work but needs someone to own it and every act to agree. Or widen to 10 and pay for it by cutting a set. The AV spec due Thursday should give real setup and strike numbers, so this can be decided from data rather than guessed.*

### 2. There is no rain plan anywhere

**Outdoors, noon to 6, in Maine, on October 3.** *Nothing in any doc, card or recap says what happens if it rains.*

**Wallace Events is already a listed partner for equipment and tenting**, so this is a scope conversation and not a new relationship. *What is actually missing is the decision structure: what threshold triggers the call, who makes it, and by when. Improvising at 8am on the day with artists already travelling is the bad version.*

### 3. Nothing exists before 12:00

**The run of show starts at noon as if the site assembles itself.** *No load-in, no line check, no vendor setup, no First Aid position, no merch table, no signage.*

*The Friday soundcheck covers sound, not setup. And the city question list already asks about vehicle access and load-in times, so the morning block cannot be written until that comes back.*

### 4. Light in the 4 to 6 block

**WaveWarZ is the visual centrepiece and the thing being streamed and photographed**, and it runs in the last two hours of daylight in early October. *A dim final battle is the single moment that can least afford to look bad.*

*Bomb Squad did lights at ZAOville - the ZAOville notes mention coordinating with them plus pool lights and tiki torches. Same crew, already have gear.*

### 5. The 6pm move has a slot but no mechanism

**Fifteen minutes is written down. Nothing else is.** *Who walks the crowd across. What is playing while they move. Who is at the door of Black Moon. Whether there is signage.*

***The entire audience has to physically relocate****.* *If it is quiet, they scatter, and the indoor half starts to a half-empty room.*

### 6. No contingency for an act not showing

**Four acts are still unconfirmed.** *If one falls through in week 39 there is no written answer. The DJ extending is the obvious move and it is not written anywhere, which means it gets improvised.*

### 7. Nobody eats

**It is a ten-hour day for crew and a long one for artists**, and there is no break window in the grid for anyone running the day. *The Black Moon gift cards exist for exactly this and are not tied to a time.*

### 8. Nothing acknowledges the daytime audience

**This sits inside Art of Ellsworth and Maine Craft Weekend**, which brings a family and craft-fair crowd through the middle of the day. *The noon to 2 programming does not reflect that anywhere. Not necessarily a change to make, but worth deciding on purpose rather than by default.*

### 9. Content capture is a goal and is not in the run of show

**Farming footage was the stated strategy from Aug 3 onward** and it appears nowhere in the day's plan. *No photo moments, no interview window, no set times for whoever is shooting. The one crew who would do it is also performing.*

### 10. The night has no end

**"20:30 to late" is the last line of the grid.** *No end time, no noise cut-off, no load-out, no who-locks-up. The city question list should probably carry the noise cut-off question.*
