## What is already settled and needs no discussion

- **The five tier names** are in circulation and are not in dispute: Presenting,
  Platform, Artist / sponsor-an-artist, Community, Friend of the Fest.
- **What each tier gets** is written and agreed - see `deck-2026-10-03.md`
  slide 9.
- **Three discount rules** hold at any price: in-kind counts at retail value with
  no extra discount stacked on top; sponsor-an-artist is never discounted because
  it is a cost and not a margin; and there is a floor below which there is no
  negotiation.
- **Nothing says tax-deductible.** There is no fiscal sponsor. The deck asks for
  sponsorship as advertising spend, which is a better ask for a bank anyway.

## The facts that should decide the numbers

**Who the buyers are.** The realistic Presenting and Platform candidates are the
Heart of Ellsworth partner banks - Franklin Savings with its new Ellsworth
branch, Bangor Federal Credit Union, Machias Savings, First National - and
several are already named Art of Ellsworth sponsors. This is not cold-pitching;
it is asking businesses that already sponsor the umbrella event ZAOstock sits
inside. The practical test for the top tier is whether a branch manager can
approve it without escalating.

**Where the money stands today.** No paying sponsors yet, a couple of warm local
leads. **$1.6k** came through Artizen last season and covered getting a majority
of artists to the event, plus a **$3,000** prize from an Artizen pool - **both
are time-delayed and neither arrives before October 3**, so Zaal is currently
fronting capital himself against money already raised. *(Aug 24 standup.)*

**The close date is constrained by print, not chosen freely.** Sponsor logos go
on the poster; the poster waits on set times; Candy's print week is the week of
September 1. So the last useful early-close is the last day a new sponsor still
reaches printed material. After that a yes buys a website logo and nothing
physical, which is not what a top tier is sold as. **Candy owns the input here:
the real question for her is the latest date she can take a sponsor logo and
still turn the poster around.**

**Finder's fees are not decided either.** The 10% / 15% / up to 25% ladder is a
proposal. Zaal, Aug 24: *"None of that's formalized or finalized."* If tier
prices get set today, this becomes answerable in the same sitting.
