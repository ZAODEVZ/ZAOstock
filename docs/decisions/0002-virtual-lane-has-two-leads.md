# 0002. The virtual lane has two leads

Aziz and Ohnahji, split by what each already does.

- **Aziz** owns the rig and Restream: encoder, bitrate, the OBS to RTMP path,
  destinations, and the stream staying up on the day.
- **Ohnahji** owns scheduling and guests: who goes on when, virtual segments
  between sets, hosts and callers, the run of the stream as a show.

**The split line: Aziz owns whether it is broadcasting, Ohnahji owns what is on
it.** If a decision touches both, Zaal breaks the tie.

Motomoto is in the virtual crew and does not lead a half.

## What this reversed

The people map finalised 24 August 2026 recorded, as Zaal's own decision, that
virtual had **no lead deliberately**: "get people together and let them suggest
the shape". It stated that the blank was an answer, not an oversight.

This decision supersedes that by date, three days later. It is written down
because a reversal that fast is worth seeing rather than quietly overwriting,
and because the earlier call was never given a file of its own to supersede.
