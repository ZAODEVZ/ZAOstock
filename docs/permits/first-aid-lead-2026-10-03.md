## 2. What the standard is, absent a City rule

Doc 1032 Finding 3, sourced to small-festival safety guidance for around 500
attendees: one certified first-aider or EMT on site, a roaming assistant with
basic first aid, a pre-confirmed mutual-aid line to local EMS with a 5-10 minute
response (not parked on site), a station that is central, visible from the
stage and reachable by an ambulance, staffed the whole show window and in place
30 minutes before doors. Equipment: AED, first-aid kit, blankets, sharps
container.
