## 3. The one open question - the Fire Chief's review

Chapter 14 has no first-aid clause of its own. What it does have is the Fire
Chief's discretion: fire or EMS personnel may be required "as a condition of
approval, at the applicant's expense" (doc 1070 Finding 2). That review is part
of the same Mass Gathering License process as the permit, and it only binds if
the Art of Ellsworth umbrella exemption does not apply - doc 1070's reading is
that it probably does not, but that is a reading (`docs/permits/status-2026-08-27.md`
row 1).

**So the one open question is: will the Fire Chief require anything of us on the
day?** Not decided here. It is the ask to the Fire Department below, and nothing
else in this page changes until it is answered. If the answer is a standby crew,
doc 1070's sourced range for a 6-hour single-ambulance standby is $1,500-2,000+,
against about $1.5K on hand - which is why the question is asked now and not in
September.

## 6. What the standard would have been, for the record

Doc 1032 Finding 3, sourced to small-festival guidance for around 500
attendees: one certified first-aider or EMT on site for the whole show window, a
roaming assistant, a pre-confirmed EMS line, a central station. Zaal's call at
200-250 in person is a contact and a kit instead. The gap between the two is the
open question in section 3, and the Fire Chief's answer is what closes it either
way.
