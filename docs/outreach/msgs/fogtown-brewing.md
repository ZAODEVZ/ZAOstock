Hi Joy,

I am Zaal, one of the organisers of ZAOstock, a free one-day music festival on
Saturday 3 October at the Franklin Street Parklet, part of the 9th annual Art
of Ellsworth and Maine Craft Weekend. Music from noon to six on the street,
then everyone walks next door to Black Moon for the evening.

I am asking Ellsworth businesses to back it. We expect 200 to 250 people on
the day and around a thousand watching online, and every act on the bill is
paid. This is a commercial sponsorship - a marketing spend, not a donation -
and what you get is your name on the day: on the parklet, in the programme, on
the site and the stream, and in the thank-you from the stage.

Two specific things beyond the sponsorship. Friday 2 October is our soundcheck
night and the artists will be in town with nowhere to be afterwards - is
Fogtown the place that night? And on the Saturday, would you want to be the
food on the parklet? No booth fee, you keep what you sell, and the crowd is
already outside your door.

Would you take fifteen minutes so I can walk you through it? I can come to
you. If it is a no, a reply saying so is a kindness.

Zaal Panthaki
ZAOstock / ZAO Festivals
info@thezao.com
