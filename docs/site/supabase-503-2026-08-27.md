## Why "503" and "quota" got attached to this

- `src/lib/lineup-fallback.ts` and the lineup route comments record a real
  event: on 2026-08-22 the org hit its egress quota and REST returned 402,
  refilling 2026-09-21. **That is not what is happening now.** 24 h of edge
  logs show zero 402s, and the anon key gets 200 in 150 ms.
- The route was then built to answer 503 whenever upstream fails and the
  fallback is empty. The fallback is empty. So every upstream failure, for any
  reason, now reads as "503". The status code is honest; the attribution to
  Supabase is not.
