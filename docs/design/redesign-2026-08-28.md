# ZAOstock redesign - page by page - for SITE

Written 2026-08-27 by the DESIGN lane. SITE implements; this file says what
each route is for, what it must carry so one link in one email answers a
sponsor, a partner, an artist or the City, which component from `DESIGN.md`
renders it, and the order to build. Zaal's words, 27 Aug: "have everything
ready so when we send the email they have all the info they can share."

Rules that apply to every route:

1. **Facts come from disk.** `src/content/festival.ts` first; then
   `docs/marketing/press-kit.md` (marketing lane branch
   `bettercallzaal/lane-marketing-0827`, not yet on main), then
   `docs/sponsor/deck-2026-10-03.md`, then
   `docs/plans/production-plan-2026-10-03.md`. A fact not in one of those is
   **UNSET** and renders as nothing, never as a guess.
2. **Locked by Zaal, 27 Aug 2026, and they override anything older on disk:**
   music starts at **noon** (not 11:00 - `festival.ts` and the production plan
   still say eleven and SITE moves them back); attendance **200-250 in
   person, 1,000 online**; the WaveWarZ battlers may be named - **Stilo, Jango,
   Lui, Quan**, with **Hurricane** on the mic; spellings **Lyons Den**,
   **Dcoop**, **Fellenz**; Steve's four acts (The Crown Vics, DJ Aquavantes,
   The Somes Sound, North Creek) are **PROPOSED** and never appear on a public
   page; there is **no fiscal sponsor** and nothing says tax-deductible except
   to say it is not.
3. **Lineup hold until 1 September.** Werb and Lyons Den are public
   (`src/app/page.tsx`). Nothing else is, until Zaal names it on the day.
4. **Prices are UNSET.** Deck slide 9 killed the $500/$1,000/$5,000 ladder on
   27 Aug. The live site still shows it on `/sponsor/deck` and `/pitch`. Tier
   names and what each gets are settled and render; prices do not.
5. **One venue at a time.** Parklet until six, then everyone walks into Black
   Moon. No page may offer a choice of two rooms. The live homepage still does.
6. **Contact:** `info@thezao.com` everywhere. The overview one-pager and
   `/donate` use `zaal@thezao.com` and `llms.txt` a Gmail address; MARKETING
   flagged it and Zaal picks. Until he does, the deck's address wins.
7. **Credit Candy** in the footer of every page that shows the badge.
8. **Glue-first.** Next 16, React 19, Tailwind 4 as they are. No component
   library, no animation library, no CMS. Theme is `@theme` tokens in
   `src/app/globals.css`; fonts are `next/font/google` in `layout.tsx`;
   markdown pages go through the `react-markdown` + `remark-gfm` path that
   `/onepagers/[slug]` already has. The only new dependency allowed is none.
