## What ZAOstock provides

- Stage, monitors, professional PA, lighting
- 1 sound engineer for the day, available for rider needs
- Backline (specific items confirmed via technical rider intake form)
- Recording of the set in audio + video, sent to the artist within 30 days
- Hospitality: water, food, basic green-room space, parking pass
- Crew shirt + day-of access for the artist plus 1 plus-one
- Inclusion in the recap reel + photo content shared after the event

## What artist provides

- Performance during the assigned 25-minute set window
- Completed technical rider intake form by **Friday 18 September 2026**
- Headshot + 1-paragraph bio for the lineup announcement (high-res, square preferred)
- Confirmation of travel availability by **Friday 18 September 2026**
- Day-of attendance and soundcheck participation
- Help share when ZAOstock posts the lineup announcement (one repost / share is enough)

## Travel + lodging

ZAOstock supports artist travel via the following channels (artist picks one, in order of preference):

1. **ZAO Festivals direct cover** - if event budget allows by **Friday 11 September 2026**, ZAOstock covers travel + 1 night hotel directly. Decided on a case-by-case basis based on event budget at that point.
2. **Per-artist crowdfund pool** - ZAOstock helps artist run a Giveth campaign for travel. (Corrected 2026-09-02: this said "a Giveth or GoFundMe campaign". No GoFundMe exists - zaostock.com/donate offers exactly two paths, PayPal and Giveth, verified live. Naming a channel we do not run in a signed memo is a promise we cannot keep.) Funds raised go directly to the artist. ZAOstock shares the campaign across our channels.
3. **Self-funded** - artist covers their own travel. ZAO Festivals provides hotel recommendations and local connections to reduce cost.

The path will be confirmed in writing by **Friday 18 September 2026**.

## Compensation

ZAOstock operates at break-even. There is no guaranteed performance fee for Year 2 (2026). Artist compensation comes from:

- 100% of any merch sold by the artist on-site (ZAOstock does not take a cut)
- 100% of any tips collected for the artist
- Recording of the set, owned by the artist, free to use commercially
- Recap content + photography credit, owned mutually
- Crowdfund support per the travel section above

For Year 3 (2027) and beyond, ZAOstock targets a guaranteed performance fee structure. Year 2 is brand-building.

## Image + recording rights

- Artist retains full ownership of their performance recording.
- ZAOstock retains the right to use audio and video clips up to 90 seconds in length for promotional content (recap reel, social posts, future event marketing).
- For commercial use beyond promotion (e.g. paid streaming distribution), parties will negotiate separately.
- Photography taken at ZAOstock by ZAO Festivals photographers is shared with the artist for free use in their own promotion.

## Cancellation

- If ZAOstock cancels the event, the artist is released with no further obligation. Any travel costs already incurred by the artist (deposits, non-refundable bookings) are the responsibility of ZAOstock to refund up to a cap of $300 per artist.
  > **Aggregate, noted 2026-09-02:** across the nine acts on the run of show this
  > is up to **$2,700** of cancellation exposure. The budget's contingency line is
  > $1,000, and whether the event policy carries cancellation cover (as distinct
  > from general liability) is recorded UNSET and is a second-round question for
  > the broker. Not a change to this clause - a number that should exist in the
  > budget and does not.
- If the artist cancels with more than 30 days notice, no penalty.
- If the artist cancels with less than 30 days notice, ZAOstock will work with them in good faith to find a solution. Repeated late cancellations affect future bookings.
- Force majeure (weather, public health, etc.): both parties act in good faith. ZAOstock may move the event to a virtual format with artist's consent.
  > **Corrected 2026-09-02.** This clause used to offer "a backup date or virtual
  > format". There is no backup date and there cannot be one: 3 October is fixed
  > inside the 9th annual Art of Ellsworth and Maine Craft Weekend, dates nobody
  > on our side controls, which is why "rain or shine" is the only honest policy
  > (`docs/permits/rain-plan-2026-10-03.md:22`). Offering a rescheduled date in a

  > reasonably hold us to.

## How to use this template

1. Copy this file. Replace `[bracketed]` placeholders with real values.
2. Send as PDF or rich-text email body to the artist (and their manager, if applicable).
3. Save the signed version to `docs/music/deals/[artist-name]-2026.md` once confirmed.
4. Update the `artists` table on ZAO STOCK Supabase: set `confirmed = true`, paste deal terms in `notes`.

## What this template intentionally does NOT include

- Specific dollar fees (no guaranteed performance fee for Year 2)
- Exclusivity clauses (artist can play other festivals)
- Equity / token grants (out of scope - separate conversation if relevant)
- Long IP rights transfers (we do not own the artist's catalog)

If a specific deal needs any of those, escalate to Zaal + FailOften before sending.
