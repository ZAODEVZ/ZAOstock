## Body

Steve - three questions, and the first one is the one that matters. I would
rather ask it bluntly than find out in September.

**1. Of the four acts on your list, which are booked and which are still being
asked?**

The Crown Vics, DJ Aquavantes, The Somes Sound, North Creek. On our side they
are all recorded as proposals, because nobody here has a yes from any of them
that we can point at. We have been building the day as if all four are in, and
two of them hold the day up: your DJ is what covers every 15-minute changeover
on a single stage, and North Creek is the whole 6 to 9 block. If either is a
lead rather than a booking, I need to know this week so we can start covering
it ourselves rather than discover it at soundcheck.

A one-line answer per act is plenty: booked, asked, or not yet asked.

**2. The Somes Sound - do they provide the indoor PA, or only their own gear
for their own set?**

Those are very different things for us. If they bring a house-capable PA, that
answers most of our indoor sound question and we plan around it. If it is their
own rig for their own set, the indoor PA is still open and we need to keep
sourcing it. Either is fine; I just need to know which.

**3. If North Creek is booked - 6:00 or 6:30?**

This one only matters once the answer to question 1 is yes, and it is your
call either way:

- **6:00** - they are already playing when the street walks in at six. No dead
  air, and outside-to-inside feels like one event.
- **6:30** - everyone gets a drink and finds a spot first, and they open to a
  full room instead of a filling one.

Until I hear back we are printing "6 to 9, in booking" with no act and no
start time, because I would rather leave it blank than guess and have you
correct it later.

One small thing tied to question 1: if the DJ is booked, the grid wants him
from about 11:45 to 6 rather than noon to 5:30, because the day now opens at
11 and the battles want music going in and out of them. Does that still work
for him and the backup?

Thank you again for offering to underwrite North Creek. It is why the evening
is one three-hour block instead of a DJ set and a scramble, and I want to get
it right rather than assume it.

Zaal
