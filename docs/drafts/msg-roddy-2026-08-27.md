Hi Roddy,

A few things in one message, and I would rather ask them all now than one at a
time as we print things.

**1. Start time.** We had the Franklin Street Parklet running noon to six on
Saturday 3 October. We now want to open at 11:00. The reason is arithmetic: five
confirmed acts, 45-minute sets with 15-minute changeovers, and noon to four only
fits four of them. Starting an hour earlier fits all five and nothing after four
moves. The street clears at six either way and the evening goes indoors at Black
Moon.

Does the parklet permit, or anything under the Art of Ellsworth umbrella, cap
how early amplified sound can start on the street? And is there a cut-off at the
other end we should be working inside? If there is an hours condition or a noise
window, tell me what it is and we will build the day around it rather than ask
for an exception. Eleven is our preference, not a requirement.

**2. The insurance certificate you asked for on the 17th.** I am lining up a
local broker, and they will need three things from the City before they can
quote properly:

- What coverage amount does the City require for an event on the parklet - a
  minimum per occurrence or aggregate you need to see?
- Does the City of Ellsworth need to be named as an additional insured? If so,
  the exact wording you want on the certificate.
- What form do you need it in - a certificate of insurance from the broker, or a
  copy of the policy - and by what date?

**3. The site, so we can draw it.** These were on the 24 August agenda and never
got reached because you could not make the call:

- Vendors on the parklet - allowed at all, and if so is there a per-vendor fee
  or permit, and a cap? We are thinking of one local food spot, nothing more.
- Capacity - everyone is outside from noon to six this year, so what number are
  we working to on the parklet?
- Load-in - vehicle access and the earliest time on the morning of the 3rd.

On power, the exact question from our AV side, because they need numbers back
rather than a yes:

> On power for the parklet on 3 October: what electrical service is available
> at the stage and along the parklet, how many circuits, what amperage each,
> and where the outlets or panel are? The summer concert series ran on
> something and we would like to run on the same. If there is a City electrician
> or a contact who knows the panel, a name is enough. And if the service is not
> sufficient for a full PA, monitors, backline and lighting through to 18:00,
> is a generator allowed on the parklet, and is there a noise or placement
> condition on one?

**4. Weather.** We are outdoors in October, so:

- Is there any weather condition on the permit - hours, tents, power?
- The concert series tent: is it still standing on 3 October after the last
  Thursday on the 1st, and could we use it? If not, we would bring a tent through

  anchored?
- If we pause or stop for weather on the day, who at the City wants a call, and
  does Ellsworth Police want to know the street is clearing early?

**Two smaller things on the same thread, so they do not get lost:**
