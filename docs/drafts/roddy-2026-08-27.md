## Body

Hi Roddy,

Two things, and I would rather ask both before we print anything than after.

**1. Start time.** We had the Franklin Street Parklet running noon to six on
Saturday 3 October. We now want to open at 11:00. The reason is arithmetic: we
have five confirmed acts, each set is 45 minutes with a 15-minute changeover, and
noon to four only fits four of them. Starting an hour earlier fits all five and
nothing after four moves. The street clears at six either way and the evening
goes indoors at Black Moon.

So: does the parklet permit, or anything under the Art of Ellsworth umbrella, cap
how early amplified sound can start on the street? And is there a cut-off at the
other end we should be working inside? If there is an hours condition or a noise
window, tell me what it is and we will build the day around it rather than ask
for an exception. Eleven is our preference, not a requirement.

**2. The insurance certificate you asked for on the 17th.** I am lining up a
local broker, and they will need three things from the City before they can
quote properly. I would rather get them from you than guess:

- What coverage amount does the City require for an event on the parklet - is
  there a minimum per occurrence or aggregate you need to see?
- Does the City of Ellsworth need to be named as an additional insured on the
  policy? If so, tell me the exact wording you want on the certificate.
- What form do you need it in - a certificate of insurance from the broker, or
  a copy of the policy itself - and by what date?

Two smaller things on the same thread, so they do not get lost:

- One of our acts involves fire spinning. The performer is handling the
  open-flame permit with the fire department directly, and we are asking him to
  carry his own fire-performer policy naming ZAOstock and the City. Is there
  anything the City wants from us on that beyond the fire department's say-so?
- The 45-day notice to the Police Chief under Chapter 14. That window closed on
  19 August if it applied to us standalone. Does the Art of Ellsworth umbrella
  cover our permit, so that it never applied? This has been open since July and
  I think you can settle it in a sentence. If a surety bond is owed, tell me the
  amount and I will get it moving.

And whenever your legal team's liability release is ready, send it over and I
will turn it around.

Thanks - happy to come by rather than do this over email if that is easier.

Zaal

## Why these questions and not others

- **The 11:00 question is the only one that touches the City and is already
  live on public surfaces.** `FESTIVAL.window` is `11 AM - 6 PM` and `/program`
  and `/pitch` both say eleven, none of it cleared. If he says noon, the day
  loses one slot and `src/content/festival.ts` is the single value that moves.
- **The three certificate questions are the three things the broker email is
  written around.** The broker cannot quote without the coverage amount and the
  additional-insured answer; the form and deadline turn a guess into a date.
  Asking Roddy directly means the broker gets the City's answer, not ours.
- **The fire line asks, it does not assert.** The performer-carries-own-COI
  structure is from doc 1045 section 3.6 and is standard practice. Dcoop has not
  yet confirmed he carries such a policy, so the draft says "we are asking him
  to", not "he will". Whether the City wants to see that COI is UNSET and this
  is where it gets asked.
- **The Chapter 14 line is unchanged from Draft 2.** Open since July, one
  sentence closes it, and the surety bond question rides on it.
- **Vendors, capacity, power and load-in are NOT in this message.** They were
  the 24 Aug agenda block that never got reached, and they are real, but they
  are a different conversation and this one should stay short enough that he
  answers it.
