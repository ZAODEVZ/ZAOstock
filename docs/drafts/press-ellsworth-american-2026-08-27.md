## What the story is

Not "a festival is coming". Three angles, any one of which stands alone. In
order of strength for a local paper:

**1. A global music community picked Ellsworth, because one of them lives
here.** The ZAO is an independent music community, 100+ members across 20+
countries (`src/app/onepagers/overview/page.tsx` PILLARS). Its last two
festivals were in New York during NFT NYC and Miami during Art Basel
(`docs/sponsor/deck-2026-10-03.md` slide 3). The third is a street in
Ellsworth, because the founder bought a house here and sits on the Heart of
Ellsworth promotions committee (deck slide 4; gdoc Aug 3 notes, "Zaal is on
the local non-profit / promotions committee"). Year 1 is deliberately about
relationship over scale (overview page hero copy). That is a local-paper
story: why here, why free, what they want from the town.

**2. The town gets a number out of it.** ZAOstock is measuring what a free
street festival does for downtown businesses - an ordinary Saturday against
3 October, published combined across businesses (`docs/plans/production-plan-2026-10-03.md`
section 7; deck slide 8). Black Moon is the first business asked. Nobody else
in the Art of Ellsworth calendar is publishing that comparison. The paper can
run the before now and the after in October.

**3. Music battles, decided by the crowd, in the street.** WaveWarZ is a live
music-battle format where two artists go head to head and the audience picks
the winner, in person and online (`src/app/program/page.tsx` BLOCKS[1]). It has
run 1,452 battles online as of 27 August (deck slide 6, measured live). On
3 October it runs on the Franklin Street Parklet from four to six. It is the
most photographable two hours of the day.

The shape of the day, for the fact box: Saturday 3 October, Franklin Street
Parklet, free, outdoors until six, then everything walks next door into Black
Moon Public House for the evening (`src/content/festival.ts`,
`src/app/program/page.tsx`). Part of the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during
Maine Craft Weekend (`src/app/page.tsx:486`). Rain or shine under tent from
Wallace Events (production plan section 4).
