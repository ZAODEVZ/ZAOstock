## Before this sends - one field

**Expected attendance is UNSET and a broker cannot quote without it.** It is the
single number that moves an event policy, and Zaal has not typed one. Note this
is the *same* figure slide 4 of the deck is blocked on, so answering it once
unblocks both.

His stated *awareness* goal has been "4,000 of the 8,000 people in Ellsworth".
That is a goal, not an attendance projection, and it must not be handed to an
underwriter as one.

## Deliberately not in the email

- **No budget figure.** K&K Insurance's published festival minimums are $2,500
  for general liability alone and $5,000 for package coverage. That is a useful
  yardstick for judging his quote and a bad thing to put in an intro email.
- **No mention of the previously-warm agency**, Brown Holmes & Milliken in
  Ellsworth. Still the fallback if this quote comes in high with less coverage;
  Lotfey Dennett in Portland is the second fallback.
- **No sponsor or budget context.** He is quoting a risk, not evaluating a
  business.
