# The 1 September artist send - one email per confirmed act

**DO NOT SEND before 1 September. Zaal sends, one per artist.** Written 30 August
because the verdict that created it had no artifact: Zaal, 29 August, on moving
the submission cutoff - *"update sept 1st, will share the flyers and artists
will get sent the artist rider form and all info they will need."*

That is three things happening on one day, and this file is the third:

1. The lineup reveal goes public (site, press release, socials).
2. Submissions close.
3. **Every confirmed act gets everything they need, in one email.**

## Why this is one email and not a form

The rider used to be a web form at `/musicians/rider`. As of 29 August it is an
email link, so nobody fills anything in on the site any more: we send the
questions, they reply. That makes this email the whole artist onboarding, and it
is the only thing standing between a confirmed act and turning up prepared.

## The email

**Subject:** ZAOstock, Saturday 3 October - your set time and everything you need

Hi [NAME],

You are on the bill. ZAOstock is Saturday 3 October on the Franklin Street
Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, it is free to get in, and the lineup went public
this morning.

**Your slot: [TIME], about 30 minutes.** The day runs one stage at a time -
outdoors from noon until six, then the whole street walks next door into Black
Moon Public House for the evening. There is no second stage and nothing
overlaps, so your set has the whole crowd.

**Friday 2 October is soundcheck night, and it is mandatory.** Every act checks
Friday evening, outside and inside. Nothing gets built on Saturday morning, so
Friday is the only time you are on that stage before the crowd is there. I will
send the running order for the evening once it is set. If Friday is genuinely
impossible for you, tell me now rather than later - it is a contract line.

**Saturday, in short:**

- On site by 10:00. The stage manager walks the whole bill at 11:30 and says the
  overrun rule out loud - if you run long, the act after you loses that time, so
  we hold it hard.
- Music starts at noon with a five-minute welcome, then sets back to back. The
  changeovers are short and held by our MC with a word from our partners; there
  is no DJ between sets this year.
- Water and power are provided at the parklet. Black Moon's basement is the
  dressing room and bathroom for performers, wristbands only, and it is not a
  hangout - we are enforcing that because Steve asked us to.
- After your set you eat: Black Moon is putting a $20 gift certificate behind
  the bar for every performer.
- WaveWarZ takes the stage from four to six. Stilo, Jango, Lui and Quan battle,
  Hurricane hosts, and the crowd decides in the street and online.

**What I need back from you, by reply.** This is the rider, and it is an email
now rather than a form, so just answer what applies:

1. The name you want on the poster and on the mic, spelled exactly.
2. Who is coming - how many people on stage, and how many with you in total.
3. What you need on stage: instruments, inputs, DI boxes, anything that is not a
   vocal mic and a guitar. Say what you bring and what you need from us.
4. Backing tracks or playback: what format, and who runs it.
5. Travel: where you are coming from, and whether you need help getting here.
6. Merch: do you want table space?
7. A link to a track or a set, if we do not already have one, for the socials.
8. Anything that would make the day go wrong for you if I did not know it.

**Two things I will send once they land:** the Friday soundcheck running order,
and load-in and parking, which I am confirming with the City this week.

Photos and video from the day come back to you afterwards, yours to use.

Anything at all, reply here or call me.

Zaal Panthaki
ZAOstock / ZAO Festivals
info@thezao.com
