## Where it lands

ArDrive, per the brief. What is needed before anything can land there:
- A ZAO ArDrive drive and a folder scheme (per event, per day, per type).
  UNSET; nothing on disk says one exists.
- Who uploads, and from which machine. The stream recording is on the
  Ellsworth desktop; photos and phone video are wherever the shooters are.
- Upload cost. ArDrive is pay-per-upload in AR; a full day of 1080p30 at 6
  Mbps is roughly 16 GB for six hours. Price UNSET, not looked up here.
