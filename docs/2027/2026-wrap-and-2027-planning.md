What this tells us for 2027:
- Count people at the door (Luma check-in, or a simple clicker count).
- Use a tracking link per channel (posters, partners, socials, newsletter) so we know what brings people.
- Run one main sign-up place instead of several, and link everything else to it.
- Turn on referral credit, so connectors are seen, thanked, and on the sponsor side paid.
- Ask one or two optional questions at sign-up (town, how you heard), so we can show sponsors who comes.

## 4. 2027: moving to a sponsor-commission model

For 2027 ZAOstock will not pay fixed fees for advisory services. Instead, people who bring in sponsors are paid a commission on what those sponsors contribute. We'd like Meridian Ridge's help designing that program, and to hear what Meridian Ridge would commit to for 2027 on that basis.

Open questions:
1. What commission structure is fair: a flat percentage, tiers by sponsor size, or a finder's fee plus a share of renewals?
2. Cash only, or in-kind sponsors too (gear, food, venue)?
3. What counts as "your" sponsor: an intro, a signed deal, or money received? Do renewals count?
4. Sponsor tiers: what each tier gets, and pricing anchors from festivals our size.
5. Pipeline: the list, the stages, who owns follow-up.
6. Which Maine and regional businesses to approach first?
7. How to track and pay commission so it is simple and fair for anyone who brings a sponsor.
8. What a sponsor needs from us to say yes (attendance, demographics, this year's data).
9. What would Meridian Ridge commit to for 2027 (pieces, time, from when), remote or on site?

## 5. Local reach: Towns Of

Towns Of (townsof.com) is a local news and events platform whose founder offered ZAO Festivals far more audience data. Questions for 2027:
- Does Towns Of cover Maine? (Its published footprint is New Jersey, the Hudson Valley and Eastern Pennsylvania.)
- What audience data can it share, and how would we use it for ZAOstock and other local events?
