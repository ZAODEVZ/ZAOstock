# ZAO Festivals 2027 sponsor program - first draft

> **Status:** DRAFT, started 18 October 2026. Nothing here is decided. Every percentage, price and tier is open until Zaal rules on it.
> **Builds on:** [2026 wrap-up and 2027 planning](2026-wrap-and-2027-planning.md), section 4, and the 2026 [finders and management fee structure](../sponsor/finders-fee-structure.md).
> **Rule for this file:** it is public. No fees, no prices, no names of private people. Figures live in finance records, not here.

## What changed for 2027

In 2026, ZAOstock paid a fixed fee for event advisory work. In 2027 it will not. People and firms who help ZAO Festivals land sponsors are paid a commission on what those sponsors contribute, so the people who help are paid out of the money they help bring in.

Meridian Ridge Event Advisory, which ran the 2026 founding-year pilot, has been asked to help design this program. This draft is the starting point for that work, not a finished proposal.

## Two layers

The program has two layers. The first is the base for 2027. The second is an option that sits on top of it.

### Layer 1 - sponsorship with commission (the base)

A sponsor pays a sponsorship, the way it does at any local festival. Whoever brought the sponsor in, and whoever runs the relationship through the event, earns a commission on what the sponsor actually pays.

This keeps the 2026 shape: one share for bringing a sponsor, a second share for managing one, and both shares for someone who does both. What is new is that an outside advisor can earn on the same terms as a teammate.

### Layer 2 - sponsor-funded rewards for attendees (option, undecided)

ZAOstock is free, so a sponsor can't buy attention through a ticket. Layer 2 gives the sponsor something to hand out on the day instead.

1. Part of the sponsorship is set aside to fund a festival rewards pool.
2. The sponsor gets an allocation of festival rewards to give away at the event.
3. The sponsor gives them out for things people do: scanning a QR code at its booth, joining an activation, or finishing a challenge (ZAOstock ran 9 challenges in 2026).
4. ZAO Festivals runs the rewards. The sponsor's staff don't need any special setup or technical knowledge to give them out.

Why it might work: the sponsor gets a reason for people to come to its booth, and a count it can see of who took part. The festival gets a pool funded by sponsors, not by ticket sales.

The details of how the rewards pool works are internal and are not in this file. Layer 2 needs a separate yes from Zaal before it is pitched to any sponsor.

## What a sponsor needs from us to say yes

The 2026 data showed the gap (see the planning doc, section 2): we can't say who came or what brought them. A 2027 sponsor will ask, so the program depends on:

- A count at the door (Luma check-in or a clicker).
- A tracking link for each channel, including each sponsor's own promotion.
- One main sign-up place.
- One or two optional sign-up questions (town, how you heard).
- If Layer 2 runs: a count of each sponsor's activations, which it can't get from any other source.

## Commission questions for the design work

These carry over from section 4 of the planning doc, narrowed to what has to be settled first.

1. **Base:** is commission paid on cash received only, or on in-kind value too (gear, food, venue)?
2. **Credit:** what counts as bringing a sponsor: an intro, a signed deal, or money received? The 2026 rule was "earliest verifiable touchpoint", with Zaal deciding disputes.
3. **Renewals:** does a second year earn anything for the person who brought the sponsor?
4. **Layer 2 split:** if part of a sponsorship funds the rewards pool, is commission calculated on the full amount or on what is left?
5. **Floor:** 2026 paid no commission below a minimum sponsorship size. Keep that rule?
6. **Tracking and payout:** one record per sponsor of who brought it in, who manages it, what it committed, what it paid and what fee was paid. That table already exists from 2026.

## Local reach

Towns Of (townsof.com) runs a sponsor product built around RSVPs: a sponsor pays per attendee, not per event, and its branding appears on the event page and in every reminder email. That is close to what Layer 2 is trying to do, and it is worth comparing before 2027 pricing is set. A separate review covers whether Towns Of reaches Maine.
