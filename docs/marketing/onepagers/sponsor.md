# ZAOstock - Saturday 3 October 2026

> **Sponsor briefing.** Contact: Zaal Panthaki, info@thezao.com. For: [name, org - UNSET]. Date: [UNSET].

## What it is

A free, one-day, artist-built music festival on the Franklin Street Parklet in
downtown Ellsworth, inside the 9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft
Weekend. Music starts at noon: independent artists on one outdoor stage, then
two hours of WaveWarZ, a live music-battle format where the audience decides,
and at six the whole street walks next door into Black Moon Public House for
the evening. Rain or shine, under tent from Wallace Events. Produced by ZAO
Festivals, the events arm of The ZAO, whose last two festivals ran in New York
during NFT NYC and in Miami during Art Basel. Partners already in: the Town of
Ellsworth, Black Moon Public House, Star 97.7, Wallace Events, WaveWarZ, COC
Concertz, ENTERACT, Web3Metal.

## Why you

Your block, on the busiest weekend of the fall. Maine Craft Weekend brings
statewide promotion and Art of Ellsworth is in its ninth year; ZAOstock puts
live music on Franklin Street inside both. It is free to attend, so nobody
chooses between your door and a ticket. And we are measuring what the day does
for downtown - an ordinary Saturday against 3 October, published combined
across the businesses that take part, Black Moon first. If it works, you have
the number. If it does not, you have that too.

## Expected scale

| | |
|---|---|
| In person | 200-250 |
| Online | about 1,000 |
| Hours | noon to six outdoors, six onward at Black Moon |
| Format | one stage at a time; MC and sponsor spots in every changeover |
| Through Ellsworth in 2025 | over four million people (gateway to Acadia) |

Figures are Zaal's, typed 27 August 2026. WaveWarZ has run 1,452 battles
online as of 27 August, with 14.23 SOL paid to artists automatically and
on-chain; re-pull before printing.

## What we bring

- Production, sound, tent, insurance and permits, a crew on site from Friday
  night, First Aid contact and kit.
- Your name in the changeovers - the MC holds every gap, and sponsor spots
  are in every one of them. That is the most sellable line on this page and
  it is deliberately not a number; the count of reads is Zaal's to state.
- Pre: named in the announcement, the daily newsletter and the 1 September
  lineup reveal. During: on-stage mentions, logo on the backdrop, presence on
  the stream. Post: named in the recap, in the published local-business
  measurement, and in the footage that keeps circulating.
- Build-in-public: the whole plan is written down and the team ships daily.

## What we'd ask

| Tier | What it gets | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Presenting | Name on the banner, the poster, the stage and the stream. Named in every announcement. Two on-stage mentions. First refusal on 2027 | **UNSET** |
| Sponsor an artist | Covers one artist's travel. They make content carrying your name. The artist opts in | **UNSET** |
| Community | Logo on the site, named in the recap, thanked from stage | **UNSET** |

Early close: **UNSET** percent off any cash tier for a signed yes by **UNSET**.
The last useful close is the last day a logo still reaches the poster, which
prints the week of 1 September.

Three rules hold at any price: in-kind counts at retail value toward a tier
with no extra discount stacked; sponsor-an-artist is never discounted, because
it is a cost and not a margin; and there is a floor below which there is no
negotiation.

Sponsorship is a marketing spend. ZAOstock has no fiscal sponsor; nothing here
is tax-deductible, and no tax receipt is issued. Money flows once: sponsor to
ENTERACT to production, 50% upfront and 50% before delivery.

## What success looks like

Your name said from the stage between sets on Franklin Street, on the backdrop
behind every performer and on the stream, and a published number in October
showing what the day did for the block you are on. And a first refusal on 2027
if you want it.

## Why now

The poster prints the week of 1 September and the lineup goes public the same
day. A yes before print reaches paper, the backdrop and every announcement; a
yes after reaches the site and the stage. The close date, once Zaal types it,
is that print date.

**One ask above all: fifteen minutes this week, then a yes or a no.**
