# ZAOstock merch and print-on-demand - what was decided, what is owed

Written 2026-08-27 (Thursday). Source: the **Aug 3 ZAOville catch-up** recap
on the Meetings tab of the Google Doc (`docs/plans/gdoc-1B78AVonJS3-snapshot-2026-08-27.md`
lines 968-1090), plus the two later mentions that change its status. Attendees
on that call: DCoop, Zaal, Profit, Paper. The recap carries its own VERIFY on
whether "Pippa" and "Prof" are the same person; not resolved here. Lane:

## Decided on 3 August

| Decision | Words from the recap |
|---|---|
| **Print-on-demand, zero stock** | "Merch = print-on-demand, zero stock, plus a scannable QR at events" |
| **Samples only at the event** | "print only a couple of samples (don't haul all the stock) + a scannable QR/table that sends people straight to the shop to order on the spot. Zero inventory to carry" |
| **Zero-overhead rule** | "don't sink money into merch when better money exists elsewhere; just need someone at a booth saying 'here, look' to drive the purchase right then (people won't 'get it later')" |
| **Shopify print-on-demand as the backup store** | "Prof: set up Shopify print-on-demand backup and graphics" - Prof "strongly recommends print-on-demand, is well-versed" |
| **Local printer for samples** | Eric, "local Ellsworth merch printer with his own press (print-on-demand + local business)... can print samples fast, Coop can wear them in streams this week" |
| **Push server is the QR** | "Modular reusable push server (build once, reuse per event)" - the QR at the table points through it |
| **Linked fashion lines exist** | "Bomb Squad + Coop's + Paper's lines are all linked now and about to drop (a real fashion line)" - relevant because Bomb Squad is now a ZAOstock partner with the role "crew, content and merch" |

   both depend on it."* The question is Roddy's: are vendors allowed on the
   parklet, fee, permit, cap. It is on the CITY lane's list for him. **No
