FOR RELEASE 1 SEPTEMBER 2026

**ZAOstock announces its lineup: a free all-day music festival on Franklin
Street, Ellsworth, Saturday 3 October**

ELLSWORTH, MAINE - ZAOstock, a free one-day music festival on the Franklin
Street Parklet in downtown Ellsworth, has announced its lineup for Saturday
3 October 2026.

Music starts at noon. Independent artists play the outdoor stage one after
another: [CONFIRMED-LIST], with more to come.

From four to six the street turns into WaveWarZ, a live music-battle format
where artists go head to head and the audience decides, in the street and
online. Stilo, Jango, Lui and Quan battle, with Hurricane on the mic.

At six, everything moves next door into Black Moon Public House for the
evening, with Stilo on the decks as the street walks in and live music to
close.

The event is free to attend and runs outdoors on the parklet from noon until
six, rain or shine under tent cover from Wallace Events. It is part of the
9th Annual Art of Ellsworth during Maine Craft Weekend, and is produced by
ZAO Festivals, the events arm of The ZAO, an independent music community with
members in more than twenty countries.

[QUOTE - Zaal]

Partners giving time, venue and infrastructure include the Town of Ellsworth,
Black Moon Public House, Star 97.7, Wallace Events, WaveWarZ, COC Concertz,
ENTERACT and Web3Metal.

[QUOTE - Black Moon]

Musicians who want to be considered for the remaining slots can submit at
zaostock.com/musicians until 3 September.
