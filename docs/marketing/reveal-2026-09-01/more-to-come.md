# "More to come" - the one line

Zaal's rule (27 Aug 21:4x): the reveal prints the acts confirmed in writing by
31 August and says "more to come" for the rest. The line, in the form each
surface uses. Never "TBA", never "and more!", never a number of acts still to
land.

| Surface | Form |
|---|---|
| Poster, under the lineup | **MORE TO COME** (Space Mono, uppercase, as a badge or a single line under the last name) |
| Poster, alt form if the strip is tight | **+ more to come** |
| Firefly / X / Farcaster | `More to come.` (its own sentence, after the names) |
| Group chats, lowercase voice | `more to come.` |
| Telegram, inline | `, more to come` (after the list, before the date) |
| Discord, LinkedIn, Facebook, prose | `with more to come` (inside the sentence that lists the acts) |
| Press release | `, with more to come.` (end of the lineup sentence) |
| Press kit lineup section | `More acts will be announced.` (the formal register) |
| Site lineup section (SITE) | `More to come.` under the list; the gold badge stays until the list is final |

What it must never sit next to: a proposed act's name, a count ("three more"),
or a date for the next announcement. None of those is a fact on disk.
