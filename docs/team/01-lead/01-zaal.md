# Zaal Panthaki, lead

Founder of The ZAO. Lead of ZAOstock 2026, and the living source of truth for
anything not yet written down.

## Owns

- Outreach, partners, sponsors, money and the city lane
- Permits, insurance, Heart of Ellsworth, local promotion
- The final say on anything public: names, dates, figures, claims

## Decides

Zaal breaks every tie. Escalate to him and do not decide alone on:

- Any sponsor close, partner ratification or money movement
- Any change to the lineup reveal date
- Any change to team structure or who leads what
- Anything that would name an unconfirmed act or partner in public

## Hand off to this person when

A decision touches two lanes and the two owners disagree. That is the tie he
exists to break, and escalating is faster than negotiating sideways.
